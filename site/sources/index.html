Controlled AI Work With XRefKit

AI work needs a goal,
bounded responsibility, and proof.

AI can focus its capabilities when the purpose, decision scope, and expected output are bounded. XRefKit defines the overall desired state and acceptance conditions as a Goal, divides the work into bounded responsibilities, and connects them until the Goal is accepted.

Goal + acceptance
→
Bounded responsibilities as Skills
↓
Semantic routing selects the next Skill
→
Workflow protocol
↓
Human acceptance / handoff

The Goal holds the overall completion criteria. Skills focus AI work, routing connects responsibilities, and the workflow protocol keeps incomplete work visible.

AI characteristics

AI performs best with a bounded purpose

  • It responds to the context and request available now.
  • It can finish a local task without knowing whether the business work is complete.
  • A clear purpose, decision scope, and expected output help it focus its capabilities.

How XRefKit connects AI work to a Goal

The Goal stays larger than any one AI task

  • The Goal defines the desired state and acceptance conditions.
  • Skills divide the work into bounded responsibilities.
  • Routing connects responsibilities, while the workflow protocol keeps incomplete work visible.
