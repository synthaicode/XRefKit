What belongs to csharp_review?

The review method is specific to this Skill. Execution management comes from the common foundation; people retain acceptance authority.

Responsibilities in a C# review
Individual Skill and its selected KnowledgeCommon foundation
Review method: establish the Roslyn baseline, avoid duplicate findings, and inspect applicable language, lifecycle, exception, synchronization, and system-boundary behavior.Start and route: capture the work instruction and its boundary, select the Skill, and record the runtime binding.
Review material: the Skill declares its Knowledge needs. Resolve the relevant C# criteria and common source-analysis criteria; those facts and rules remain in Knowledge.Reference delivery: discover and resolve the selected Knowledge by XID and preserve its identity in the execution record.
Review output: category results, severity, supporting evidence, rationale, remediation direction, unconfirmed items, and the next owner for out-of-scope findings.Record and check: track work items, artifacts, concerns, judgments, and handoff. Deterministic verify and close check the workflow record; they do not judge whether the review findings are correct.

An independent quality review assesses the output when required. A person reviews the findings and evidence, decides which remediation to adopt, and accepts the result or returns it for repair.
