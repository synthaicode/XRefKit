# IPA reconstruction guide mapping

This reference maps the human-oriented IPA guidance to the brownfield workflow.
It is an external reference and does not replace the repository contract.

## Source

IPA, [システム再構築を成功に導くユーザガイド 第2版](https://www.ipa.go.jp/archive/publish/secbooks20180223.html).

## Mapping to this Skill

| IPA perspective | Brownfield workflow use |
|---|---|
| Purpose, current-system state, and new-system requirements | Requirement validation and evidence separation |
| Reconstruction method and risk identification | Three-way specification reconciliation and delta classification |
| Preventive measures in planning | Initial work policy followed by post-reconciliation detailed planning |
| User/development organization agreement | Human gates, owners, evidence, handoffs, and residual-risk decisions |
| Ambiguous current specification as a risk | `unknown` with impact, resolver, and owner |

AI may organize evidence and propose work, tests, and controls. Human approval
is required for purpose, protected invariants, delta meaning, risk acceptance,
release conditions, and unresolved business or expected-result decisions.
