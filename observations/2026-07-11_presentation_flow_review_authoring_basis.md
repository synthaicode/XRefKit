# Presentation Flow Review Authoring Basis

## Observed Revision Trigger

Deck 055 required repeated structural revisions because the original slide order
introduced mechanisms before their explanatory premises were established.

The observed corrections were:

- replace an abstract "AI organization" framing with the concrete operating
  outcome: continuous business execution
- distinguish prompt-centric state loss from the AI tendency to treat a stopped
  response as completed work
- establish Goal as the definition of business completion before introducing
  task decomposition
- introduce bounded responsibilities before semantic routing, then explain
  routing as the connection between multiple responsibilities and the Goal
- place workflow protocol as the control that records progress, incomplete
  work, and handoff for each responsibility
- establish the difference between general model knowledge and
  organization-specific Knowledge before introducing XID-backed selection

## Reusable Finding

An explanatory deck must make the premise for each mechanism explicit before
introducing that mechanism. Slide-level clarity is insufficient when the
causal bridge between adjacent slides is absent, duplicated, or inverted.
